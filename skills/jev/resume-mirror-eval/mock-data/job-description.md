# Senior Platform Engineer

**Northwind Ledger** (fictional company, used for demonstration only)
Remote (US) | Full-time | Platform Engineering

## About the role

Northwind Ledger builds payment reconciliation software for mid-market finance teams. The platform team owns the infrastructure, deployment tooling and observability stack that every product team ships on. We are looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to design and operate the Kubernetes platform that runs our transaction processing services, and to raise the reliability bar across the company.

## What you will do

- Design, build and operate our production Kubernetes platform on AWS EKS, including cluster lifecycle, upgrades and multi-region failover.
- Own our infrastructure as code using Terraform and Terragrunt, with reusable modules consumed by product teams.
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions and ArgoCD, enabling safe, frequent deployments with automated rollback.
- Define and track SLOs for critical services and lead the observability practice using Prometheus, Grafana and OpenTelemetry.
- Implement service mesh capabilities with Istio for mTLS, traffic shaping and progressive delivery.
- Manage secrets and identity using HashiCorp Vault and AWS IAM with least-privilege access patterns.
- Partner with security to maintain our SOC 2 Type II and PCI DSS compliance posture through automated controls and evidence collection.
- Participate in a follow-the-sun on-call rotation and lead blameless postmortems for production incidents.
- Mentor engineers across product teams on cloud-native best practices and platform adoption.

## What we are looking for

- 6+ years of experience in infrastructure, SRE or platform engineering roles.
- Deep hands-on experience operating Kubernetes in production at scale, ideally on EKS.
- Expert-level Terraform skills and a strong opinion on how to structure infrastructure as code for multiple teams.
- Proficiency in Python or Go for building internal tooling and automation.
- Experience designing CI/CD pipelines with GitOps workflows using ArgoCD or Flux.
- Strong understanding of observability principles, including metrics, tracing and structured logging.
- Experience with service mesh technologies such as Istio or Linkerd.
- Familiarity with compliance frameworks such as SOC 2 and PCI DSS in a regulated environment.
- Excellent written communication skills and a track record of documenting systems for other engineers.

## Nice to have

- Experience with FinOps and cloud cost optimization on AWS.
- Contributions to open source projects in the cloud-native ecosystem.
- Experience running PostgreSQL or Kafka in Kubernetes.

## Compensation and benefits

Base salary range: $175,000 to $215,000 depending on experience, plus equity. Fully remote within the United States. Health, dental and vision coverage; 401(k) match; a home office stipend; and a learning budget.
